Chapter 01

GOOD MORNING

P3

LISTEN | GOOD MORNING

Kanye West - Good Morning (Clean / Non-explicit)

I use this track as my start signal when I need energy and focus.

Rubric A

Good Morning: Song Description

  • I chose "Good Morning" by Kanye West, from Graduation (2007, hip-hop).
  • I see Kanye as an artist who mixes personal ambition with big, layered production ideas.
  • I hear this as an opening statement song: wake up, step up, and move into a new chapter.
  • I marked this as Fact check because sample credit details can vary slightly across sources.

Rubric B

Good Morning: Mood + Message

  • I feel happy, upbeat, and a little nostalgic when I hear it.
  • I hear momentum, not chaos. It sounds focused and forward.
  • To me the message is simple: I should start, keep moving, and build confidence through action.

Rubric C

Good Morning: Lyric + Personal Connection

"Good morning, look at the valedictorian."

  • I interpret "valedictorian" as becoming the best version of myself, not only being the top student.
  • This lyric matches my values: growth, creativity, and uniqueness.
  • Because I overthink and procrastinate sometimes, I use this line as a reminder to start now.

Rubric D

Good Morning: Now vs Future

  • This song reflects both my present and my future.
  • Now: I am practicing consistency and building momentum by starting faster.
  • Future: I want disciplined habits to feel natural so progress becomes my normal standard.

Chapter 02

TAKE FIVE

P10

LISTEN | TAKE FIVE

The Dave Brubeck Quartet - Take Five

I listen to this when I want calm focus and better choices, not just louder effort.

Rubric A

Take Five: Song Description

  • I chose "Take Five" by The Dave Brubeck Quartet (written by Paul Desmond).
  • I classify it as cool jazz from the Time Out period, commonly dated to 1959.
  • I hear it as proof that unusual structure can still feel clean and approachable.
  • I marked this as Fact check because some sources highlight 1961 for chart momentum.

Rubric B

Take Five: Mood + Message

  • I hear calm confidence and controlled creativity.
  • I hear the track as minimal, but every choice feels intentional.
  • I take its message this way: originality is strongest when it is disciplined, not forced.

Rubric C

Take Five: Musical Element + Personal Connection

  • I notice that it can feel like 4/4 at first, but when I count it, the 5/4 pattern appears.
  • I see my work similarly: it can look standard at first, but closer attention reveals my choices and effort.
  • I aim for "different done well" by using limited tools intentionally, not by over-designing.

Rubric D

Take Five: Now vs Future

  • I feel this mostly reflects how I live now: subtle creativity and originality matter to me.
  • I want my future work to keep that same quiet depth so quality increases the closer people look.
  • The 5/4 idea reminds me that structure can carry personality without being loud.
