MY PERSONAL SOUNDTRACK

Jovan

Two songs, one direction: keep growing while staying original in subtle ways.

Intro

Why I built this as a site

Making this project as a website let me express two messages from the songs I chose. Good Morning feels like a fresh start - the kind of energy that pushes you to get up and improve. Take Five shows another side of me: doing something different in a way that still feels natural and high-quality, not different just to be loud.

Building a site also matches how I like to create. I am a fast learner who thinks outside the box, and for you as my teacher, this format shows not just what songs I picked but how I think through choices and effort behind the final result.

CHAPTER 01

GOOD MORNING

by Kanye West

Spotify — Non-explicit version

Why this song?

I chose Good Morning by Kanye West from the album Graduation (2007), a hip-hop track with a confident but uplifting sound. It’s upbeat and makes me feel like I should get up and do something. What stands out to me is that it opens the album with a fresh start feeling, and it introduces the graduation idea as more than school - it’s about moving into a new level of growth. That opening-chapter energy connects to how I think about improving: you don’t wait to feel ready, you start moving and you become better through the work.

Kanye is known for blending personal ambition with bold production choices, and this opener sets that tone clearly.

What does it push me toward?

Good Morning really makes me strive to be better. I like having a goal in front of me and doing what I can to reach it. The message I take from it is not just “be successful,” but “keep improving.” It’s about building momentum and becoming the best version of yourself through consistency, not luck.

As a fast learner, that momentum matters because it helps me move from overthinking into action.

"Good morning, look at the valedictorian."

This line stands out to me because I don’t take valedictorian as only a school thing. To me it represents a better version of myself that I’m trying to reach. It connects to my values of growth and uniqueness - pushing to improve, but doing it in my own way.

I also know I sometimes procrastinate by thinking too long instead of starting. This lyric feels like a wake-up call: stop waiting, start now, and earn the version of yourself I’m aiming for.

How does it represent me right now?

This song reflects me now because I’m focused on growing and building consistency so that my future is easier and stronger. I don’t want improvement to be random - I want it to be something I build. Good Morning matches that mindset because it feels like choosing momentum on purpose.

CHAPTER 02

TAKE FIVE

The Dave Brubeck Quartet

Spotify embed

Why this piece?

I chose Take Five by The Dave Brubeck Quartet. It’s often described as cool jazz from the Time Out era (1959). The piece uses an unusual 5/4 time signature, which is even referenced in its name. What’s interesting is that it still feels smooth and clean - it doesn’t sound weird just because it’s different. The difference is subtle, and that’s what makes it so good.

The group is known for exploring unusual time signatures, and this piece is one of the clearest examples of that approach.

How can a song with no words say so much?

Take Five has a big message to me, even though it has no lyrics. It’s unique, but that uniqueness doesn’t make it awkward to listen to. It feels high-quality and controlled, but not robotic - it still has personality. It sounds disciplined, but it still feels full and alive. That balance is something I respect.

What makes it different without trying too hard?

It actually took me a long time to notice that this piece was in 5/4 and not 4/4. Even so, you can still tell there’s something special about it when you listen - you feel the difference before you can explain it.

That represents how I like to do things differently. I think outside the box, but I don’t want to add a bunch of extra things just to impress people. I’d rather use the tools I have intentionally and make a few details really strong. I like staying within what still feels normal, but adding something that makes it quietly stand out.

Does it match how I live now, or who I want to be?

This song represents how I live right now. I appreciate originality that’s subtle and thoughtful, and I try to do things differently for a reason - not just to be different. Take Five proves you can stand out without being messy, and that’s the kind of creativity I want in my own work.

Why these two songs together?

  • Good Morning is my “momentum” song — growth, fresh starts, and pushing forward.
  • Take Five is my “quiet originality” song — different, but controlled and high-quality.
  • One uses lyrics to make the message obvious; the other uses structure to make you feel it.
  • Together they describe how I want to grow: improve constantly, but stay original.
