CLE 10

MY PERSONAL SOUNDTRACK

BY JOVAN

Two songs, one direction: keep growing while staying original in subtle ways.

Intro

Why I built this as a site

While making this project, I got to show how I like to do things: use the tools I have in a smart way, then keep refining until it’s actually good. I liked being able to match the way I built the project with the messages I wanted to show through the songs, and still make something that stood out.

I don’t mean “lazy” like not trying — more like finding the most efficient way to get a high-quality result. Making this a website let the format be part of the message instead of just putting text on slides.

CHAPTER 01

GOOD MORNING

by Kanye West

Spotify — Good Morning (non-explicit)

Song details

Why this song?

I chose “Good Morning” by Kanye West from the album Graduation (2007). It’s upbeat and makes me feel like I need to get up and do something. Even though it’s about graduating, the deeper meaning to me is growing as a person and moving forward.

Moods/messages

What does it push me toward?

“Good Morning” represents how I like to grow and use my tools to make stuff at a high level. The upbeat production and lyrics make me feel like I can achieve what I want if I actually commit to it. I don’t want improvement to happen randomly — I want it to be something I build intentionally. “Good Morning” matches that mindset.

Lyric + identity

"Good morning, look at the valedictorian."

This line stands out to me because I don’t see “valedictorian” as only a school thing. To me, it represents a better version of myself that I’m trying to reach. It connects to my values of growth and uniqueness: improving while still doing it in my own way.

I also know I sometimes procrastinate by overthinking instead of starting. This lyric feels like a wake-up call: stop waiting, start now, and earn the version of myself I’m aiming for.

Now/Future

How does it represent me right now?

This song reflects me right now because I’m focused on growing and building consistency so my future is easier and stronger. I don’t want improvement to be random — I want it to be something I build on purpose. “Good Morning” matches that mindset because it feels like choosing momentum every day.

CHAPTER 02

TAKE FIVE

The Dave Brubeck Quartet

Spotify — Take Five

Song details

Why this piece?

“Take Five” has always stood out to me because it uses a 5/4 time signature, which makes it different from most songs, but the difference feels subtle rather than obvious. The instrumentation is minimal, which lets you focus on the individual musical parts instead of being overwhelmed by how much is going on.

Moods/messages

How can a song with no words say so much?

It reminds me that I don’t just want to be different for attention — I want to be creative in a way that is controlled and precise. I value doing something well with intention, where it has personality without just brute-forcing extra work.

Identity/values

What makes it different without trying too hard?

It took me a long time to notice that this piece is in 5/4 instead of 4/4. Even so, I could tell something about it was different before I could explain why.

That represents how I like to do things differently. I don’t want to add extra details just to impress people. I’d rather use the tools I have intentionally and make a few details really strong. I like staying within what still feels normal, while adding something that makes it quietly stand out.

Now/Future

Does it match how I live now, or who I want to be?

This song represents how I think right now. I appreciate originality that is subtle and thoughtful, and I try to do things differently for a reason — not just to be different. “Take Five” proves you can stand out without being messy, and that’s the kind of creativity I want in my own work.

Why these two songs together?

“Good Morning” is the momentum side of me — fresh starts, energy, and pushing myself to improve. “Take Five” is the quieter side — subtle originality, discipline, and quality without doing too much. Together they describe how I want to grow: keep improving while staying original.

Unit reflection

While making this project, I got to show how I could make something outside the box and still do a good job. I really enjoyed how I could match the way I did my project with the messages I wanted to show about myself through the songs.

Good Morning shows the side of me that likes to grow and do stuff at a high level. The upbeat sound selection and lyrics make me feel like I can achieve whatever I want to if I just put my mind to it. While it is about graduating, the song’s deeper meaning is graduating as a person so I can move on and grow. I do not want improvement to be random. I want it to be something I build on purpose. Good Morning matches that mindset because it feels like choosing momentum every day.

Take Five has always stood out to me. It uses a 5/4 time signature unlike almost any other song written. It makes it feel different, but subtly. The instrumentation is very minimal, with just a few instruments playing at once, but it is very creative, which lets you focus on the individual musical parts rather than being blown away with how much is going on. It reminds me that I don’t like doing stuff differently just for attention. I like doing it in a fun and creative way without doing too much, but still in a way that is precise.

Making this as a website allowed me to combine everything I wanted to do without having too many different things going on at once. I could put everything I wanted in a creative and fun way while making it look cool, but still having it fit the whole theme of the project.
